# Go-to-market experiment

## First demonstration

Use the unchanged HIV replay to explain the workflow, followed by a clearly labelled proposed operational scenario. Show an initial known set, an accepted batch, pending privacy, delivered labels, the budget ledger and a matched comparison. Keep the all-fallback limitation visible. A good demonstration shows how a decision is reconstructed even when no new active is found.

Use this positioning sentence: “CuraLoop helps research teams replay and compare molecular screening decisions under declared constraints, with a record of what was known at each step.” Follow it with the pilot deliverables and evidence limits. Do not imply the tool autonomously discovers a safe or effective medicine.

## Pilot funnel

Track eligible account, permission to contact, discovery conversation, data feasibility, scoped evaluation, commercial commitment, installed use, repeat use and continuation decision. Define the denominator at every stage. A website visitor or GitHub star is awareness evidence, not qualified demand. No funnel metrics exist yet.

## Sales materials

The minimum materials are a two-page problem/use-case brief, a public-data demo, an evidence/limitations sheet, the pilot offer and an installation/data-handling note. Prepare customer-specific proposals only after discovery. The manuscript and benchmark can support technical due diligence; they do not replace a statement of work or buyer fit.

## Objection handling

If a buyer already uses BayBE, BoTorch or an internal platform, propose a specific replay/reporting adapter only when a real gap remains. If data cannot leave the site, assess local installation within an explicitly agreed access boundary. If the buyer demands prospective savings proof, offer an evaluation design and identify the evidence needed. If enterprise security or regulated qualification is mandatory immediately, the current POC may not meet that purchase context.

## Business decision record

After each authorized engagement, record the actual problem, existing alternative, delivery effort, unresolved objection and next commitment. Continue a segment only if evidence supports a repeatable offer at a sustainable delivery cost. Do not publish customer names, logos, testimonials or numerical savings without accurate source records and permission.

## Revised evidence-led narrative

Demonstrate an actual decision trace and a reproducible report before discussing model sophistication. Explain which practical gap remains relative to existing backtesting/BO tools. Use a public task to evaluate that gap without requiring early proprietary-data disclosure. Report customer-measured analyst effort separately from simulated assay exposure. Do not use a publication draft or BayBE's case-specific efficiency examples as evidence of CuraLoop ROI.

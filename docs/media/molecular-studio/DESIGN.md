# Molecular Studio walkthrough

## Style Prompt
An evidence-led view of the running CuraLoop-RL workbench. Use authentic captured
screens as the visual subject. Graphite navigation, ivory canvas and copper
emphasis follow the approved Molecular Studio design, not official GPO identity.
The film is an animated walkthrough of separate actual captures, not a continuous
agent recording or evidence of scientific efficacy.

## Colors
- Canvas: #F6F4EF
- Ink: #28282B
- Secondary text: #6A6661
- Emphasis: #A6492D
- Dividers: #D9D5CF

## Typography
Use the bundled Noto Sans Thai font for English and Thai. Titles 52px, supporting
copy 30px, footer24px at1920x1080. Screens retain their authentic typography.

## Motion
Four five-second scenes. Calm0.45-second crossfades and0.55-second caption
entrances. No numeric interpolation or chart animation. A deterministic paused
GSAP timeline controls every animation. No sound, external assets or paid calls.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not invent assay results, molecules, metrics or successful integration.
- Do not recolor molecular atoms or alter captured source values.
- Do not use teal, green gradients, decorative glows or particle backgrounds.
- Do not imply that a metadata readout is an activity measurement.

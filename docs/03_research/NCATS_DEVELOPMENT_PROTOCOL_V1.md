# NCATS three-assay development pilot v1

Status before execution: DEVELOPMENT ONLY. Prepared 4 November 2026. Numerical
allocation is frozen by `research/configs/ncats_development_v1.json` and its
canonical JSON SHA256 receipt before any real-assay method outcomes are read.
This is a bounded feasibility pilot, not the final study in PROTOCOL.md.

## Source selection and rights

Use only NCATS Tox21 Challenge 2014 **training** exports for AhR (PubChem AID
743122), PPAR-gamma (743140), and p53 (720552). The [official training table](https://tripod.nih.gov/tox21/challenge/data.jsp)
links those assays and defines 1 as active and 0 as inactive. Preserve that mapping;
PPAR-gamma is a signaling agonism endpoint. A positive pathway/toxicology assay
label is not therapeutic efficacy, direct binding, or a desirable drug property.

The [NCATS provider presentation, May 2020](https://ntp.niehs.nih.gov/sites/default/files/iccvam/meetings/iccvam-forum-2020/ppt/13-ncats-simeonov-508.pdf)
states that Tox21 data are in the public domain (physical pp. 4 and 7). This
supports this local public-data research use; it is not a new CC BY grant. The
challenge exports are unversioned: retrieved bytes, timestamps and SHA256 in
`source-receipts.json` define this release. Raw and full derived pools stay in
ignored `research/raw_data/`; no challenge test/final files are used.

LIT-PCBA was considered but deferred because explicit file-level rights were not
verified here and its PPARG inhibitor endpoint differs from the selected Tox21
agonism endpoint. VDR phenotype and the R05 mutual-information direction remain
unresolved in the discrepancy register. No caption-based label inversion or MI
reproduction is attempted. PubChem pages requiring browser verification and the
NCATS disclaimer denial were not bypassed. Assay IDs above come from the public
NCATS training table; full independent PubChem descriptions were not all read.

## Cohorts and preprocessing

The three actual raw exports contain 8,169 / 8,184 / 8,634 rows respectively.
Canonicalize with RDKit 2026.09.1 isomeric SMILES; do not strip salts, normalize
tautomers or remove stereochemistry. Exclude an entire exact-canonical group
when its binary labels conflict. Collapse consistent duplicates, preserve source
aliases, exclude invalid SMILES, and reject unexpected outcome codes. Missing
labels are excluded rather than interpreted as inactivity.

Select 600 unique compounds per assay by SHA256 ranking of version, fixed seed,
assay and canonical structure, without using activity in selection. Morgan radius
2 / 1,024-bit nonchiral fingerprints are public features. The actual subsets
contain 59 / 18 / 31 actives. All three endpoints and these chemistry pools are
now exposed development resources and cannot become untouched final holdouts.

The saved preparation audit reports all attrition, empty/nonempty Murcko
scaffolds and overlap. There is no exact canonical overlap with the historical
HIV regression pool, but chemical similarity remains. The three NCATS subsets
share 36-52 exact structures pairwise and related scaffolds; treat them as three
correlated endpoints from one panel, not independent source datasets. Never feed
another endpoint's labels or the HIV labels into a fitted campaign predictor.

## Frozen allocation and estimands

- Seeds 0, 1, 2; uniform initial 24 labels from sorted IDs, without inspecting
  activity. Keep every zero-active start; initial labels are sunk knowledge and
  excluded from new-hit counts and the new-assay budget.
- Synthetic unit cost 1, budget 24, capacity 3, integer deadline T=12.
  Decision opportunities are 0..11 for every method. Settle all due events
  atomically before refilling; deliveries exactly at T count; no new action at T.
  Accepted unfinished work retains its reserved commitment at the deadline.
- Common duration 2 versus heterogeneous durations {1,2,4}, generated by the
  foundation's SHA256 keyed schedule from task/seed/candidate/assay type, never
  activity or request order. Each policy gets its own campaign and fitted state.
- Six methods x three endpoints x three seeds x two conditions = **108 runs**.
  Initial canary is the included 18 seed-0 heterogeneous runs; verify correctness
  and measured feasibility before the remaining 90, without retuning parameters.
- CPU only, concurrency 1, numerical thread limit 1, at most 300 cumulative
  run-wall seconds across both phases, checked between runs (soft by one run).
  Preserve completed/failed artifacts; no reset of the attempt denominator.

Primary development diagnostic: paired pending-penalty minus Greedy H(T), the
unique new active labels delivered by T. Also export delivered count, spent,
reserved and uncommitted units, pending/missing outcomes, left-step time-discovery
area, and new-active scaffold counts with empty scaffolds reported separately.
Time-discovery area is the foundation integral over 0..T, in hit*time units,
without cost normalization. Seeds are within-task repetitions; report descriptive
paired values, not a three-target population CI or a final H1 test.

## Controls and information boundary

Random draws uniformly without replacement from feasible candidates. Frozen
Greedy fits initial labels only; retrained Greedy refits when revealed labels
change. Both use StandardScaler -> LogisticRegression (C=1, liblinear,
max_iter=500, random_state=seed); scaler and classifier never fit hidden labels.
A one-class frozen start keeps Random fallback for the entire campaign. Dynamic
Greedy retries fitting only after new labels. Nearest-hit maximizes Tanimoto to
revealed actives, including initial actives; with no active it uses a logged
Random fallback. Diversity maximizes minimum distance from revealed references;
there is no additional within-batch diversity adjustment.

Pending penalty uses the **same** fitted predictor as the Greedy family but its
own independent state: probability - 0.2 * max Tanimoto to current pending
locations. Pending actual labels and private durations are unavailable to the
selector. The treatment does not diversify within a simultaneously submitted
batch. All methods share observed/pending duplicate exclusion, quotes, capacity,
decision grid and tie-breaking by candidate ID. One-class fallback draws are
paired across Greedy/penalty. Common-delay Greedy/penalty public event histories
must match exactly: no pending work remains at refill opportunities. A mismatch
invalidates interpretation until diagnosed; it is not evidence of an advantage.

Selectors receive immutable public Observation only; Task retains private truth.
The evaluator alone reads full labels for source audits and replay delivery.
Fits, inference, decisions, fallback requests/reasons, warnings, failures, exact
initial IDs, schedule and code digests, journals and restoration checks are saved.
Learned-controller requests/fallback are zero and N/A for these fixed controls;
model/selector Random fallback is a separate quantity.

## Limits and reproduction

Policy latency does not advance the synthetic assay clock. Report measured
CPU/wall time and peak runner working set separately. No paid API or GPU is used;
electricity and analyst labor costs are unmeasured. No remote GPU allocation is
implied by this CPU run. These six inexpensive controls omit a genuine
objective-matched asynchronous probabilistic method, ensemble uncertainty and a
learned controller. The penalty is a finite-pool heuristic, not a reproduction of
PLAyBOOK, GP-BUCB or BayBE. This pilot cannot establish held-out-target transfer,
method superiority, laboratory savings, clinical efficacy or commercial value.

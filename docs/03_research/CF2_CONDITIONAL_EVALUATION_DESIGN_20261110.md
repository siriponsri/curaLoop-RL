# Conditional evaluation design after CF2 review

10 November 2026. **NOT_RECOMMENDED_FOR_IMMEDIATE_EXECUTION; NOT_RELEASED**.
This satisfies the CF2-Design requirement to make a future comparison and its
cost reviewable. The current [NO_GO design ticket](CF2_DESIGN_DECISION_20261110.md)
means this programme does not start. There is no surviving approved new selector.

## Entry condition

Before considering this protocol, the owner and a qualified scientific reviewer
must identify one specific computational strategy that goes beyond the two
generic mechanisms, state its assumptions and distinguish it from the closest
implementable controls. A new theorem is not mandatory; a clear, useful
computational contribution with appropriate evidence is. Merely replacing a
probability estimator or naming a composition is not enough on its own.

No human reviewer was nominated or contacted in LR-CF2P. This entry is a later
owner/scientific decision, not an implementer-certified GO.

## Data and information scope for a possible development study

- Propose only the same six previously exposed CF1 campaigns initially. Reopen
  private rows/outcomes only through a new explicit grant. All six remain
  development, with observation support and AID/provenance conflicts preserved.
- Use primary measurements and public structures/groups only where the admitted
  source supports them. Hold out confirmation labels within development folds
  before fitting any calibrator or joint model. Cross-fit all preprocessing that
  uses labels. Do not resample individual rows across exposure/dependence roles.
- Evaluate on the supported primary-hit universe; retain missing outcomes as
  unsupported. Full-library claims require a different observation/admission
  design, not inverse weighting without known propensities or convenient zeros.
- Freeze predictors, grouping, observation timing, source versions, seeds,
  controls, resources and all stopping rules before evaluating the new method.
  A fitted joint law requires a checkable likelihood/information contract and
  development diagnostics beyond marginal calibration.

Primary reporting: campaign-level paired differences in H and D over the fixed
budgets, accompanied by D(FP), H/B, D/B, calibration/model diagnostics and actual
CPU. Report all budgets and unfavorable arms, not a selected best setting.
The five existing seeds do not create biological replication. Equal-campaign
means and seed ranges remain descriptive because the current cohort has one
annotation-connected component. Do not report a cross-family confidence
interval from that cohort.

For any later independent claim, first admit a new appropriate confirmation
population with independent units, source/rights/observation support and an
unexposed protocol. The old FS-Mol final16 measure a different setting; they
are not automatically the required primary-to-confirmation panel and remain
sealed. No size or power claim is made before that population is identified.

## Controls and resolution tests

| Question | Required matched comparison | What would support the specific claim |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Does primary-to-confirmation calibration help? | Same ranking/optimizer with versus without admitted calibration | Better held-out development calibration and declared downstream tradeoffs; not evidence of optimizer novelty |
| Does dependence modelling help? | Independent and joint predictors inside the same optimizer, with cost included | Useful paired effects and credible conditional diagnostics; no oracle joint information |
| Does the computational strategy help? | New strategy versus expected-coverage greedy and a closest active-search adaptation, using the same predictor and observations | Useful incremental effects at matched information/resources that survive the fixed ablations |
| Does adaptation help? | Adaptive and frozen-feedback versions of the same method | Improvement attributable to delivered feedback, not extra labels or a different budget |
| Does the finding generalize? | Separately admitted independent confirmation units | Uncertainty appropriate to those units and no outcome-selected enrolment |

No meaningful-effect or acceptable-hit-loss threshold is invented from CF1.
The owner must choose the operational tradeoff before a confirmatory protocol;
until then report effects and uncertainty. No p < 0.05 rule is introduced here.
If a cheap matched control explains the gain, record that result and stop the
new-method claim rather than adding a third candidate or tuning the gate.

## Provisional cost, not an approved account

If the entry condition is later met, a possible **development-only upper
envelope** is 16 CPU-hours / 48 elapsed hours / max two combined workers:

| Stage | Proposed ceiling | Admission / stop rule |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Source/support and technical profiling | 2 CPU-h | No molecular study before support, visibility and measured cost fit; recost with owner if they do not |
| One fixed model/strategy development fit | 4 CPU-h | One method and one frozen configuration, no extra tuning ladder |
| Matched controls and fixed ablations | 6 CPU-h | Entire frozen matrix fits remaining budget; partial results cannot pass the scientific claim |
| Analysis, checks, complete return and delivery | 3 CPU-h | Preserve failure/negative evidence and scope; no automatic independent panel |
| Technical retry reserve | 1 CPU-h | At most two identical-context infrastructure retries per unit; retained charges |
| **Total** | **16 CPU-h** | Fresh explicit release required |

This is a provisional ceiling, not a measured runtime forecast. The exact method
and matrix do not yet exist, so the first profiling stage is mandatory. Past
CF1 fit costs do not certify the runtime of an unspecified new conditional
model or nonmyopic solver. Independent evaluation, new source intake, a real
team connector, deployment and submission are outside this possible envelope.

**Current recommendation remains: do not release this study on the two generic
CF2 claims. Return to the owner after the combined product/design closeout.**

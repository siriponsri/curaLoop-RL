## Two corrections needed before execution

**An activity row is not a verified measurement.** Identifiers, relation and
units establish nominal support. A trusted check of a finite positive recorded
number and validity/duplicate flags establishes only recorded numeric support.
Even this does not certify experimental measurement, construct/procedure
equivalence or absence of imputation. Source provenance and adjudication remain
separate. No missing cell becomes an inactive label.

The report calls the pilot metadata-only while requesting measured intersections,
numeric relations and validity checks. Its handback does not fully specify the
numeric-presence access boundary. The [concrete pilot scope](../06_execution/R0_QP_PILOT_SCOPE_20261108.md)
therefore requests explicit count-only trusted validity access, with values and

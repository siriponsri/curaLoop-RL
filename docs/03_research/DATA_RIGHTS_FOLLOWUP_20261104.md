# Data rights followup

4 November 2026. Public policy/provider pages were checked during the main
delivery. No final outcome file was requested and no task was promoted to
final-ready. This is source evidence, not a legal certification.

The [NCBI policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) permits
redistribution of government-created public-domain information, while expressly
noting that PubChem can incorporate contributed/licensed material with its own
conditions. The [PubChem download guidance](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/assay/assaydownload.cgi)
also directs users to the contributor's current licensing information. These
statements do not resolve every selected source file or contributed structure.

The [UF Scripps MLPCN archive](https://hts.scripps.ufl.edu/our-capabilities/mlpcn-archive/)
describes its listed campaigns as public domain. This is useful provider-level
evidence for the Scripps search candidate, but exact campaign/AID/file mapping
and the structure-source scope still require a retained grant record. It is
not a license for unrelated NCGC assays or a prepared LIT-PCBA release.

[NCATS' data guidance](https://ncats.nih.gov/data?page=103) explains that primary
qHTS actives may reflect assay interference, samples can be mixtures and AC50
estimates can have fitting artifacts. Access and analysis permission therefore
do not establish validated target binding or therapeutic efficacy.

The [candidate audit](CANDIDATE_ASSAY_AUDIT_V1.md) remains the exact received-file
and exposure record. Close applicable grants/attribution and endpoint mappings,
whole-pool/cross-group overlap and family-role decisions before final outcomes.
No access challenge, denial or TLS error was bypassed. No new license conclusion
is inferred from a paper's software or publication license.

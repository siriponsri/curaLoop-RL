## Main findings

**1. Complete allowed metadata already distinguishes important contexts.**
The ghrelin cases name COS-7 cells, inositol-related functional context and
different residue-position anchors in their descriptions. For example, the
three admitted cases contain Ser174, Phe220 and Arg283 anchors. These are context
clues, not verified full mutation identities. The retained methods confirm the
general cell/pathway family but do not map each specific construct to a unique
procedure. Hiding those already available metadata clues would weaken the
baseline artificially.

**2. The only admitted same-target group has one molecular alternative.**
CHEMBL1001334, CHEMBL1001339 and CHEMBL1001341 each have one admitted projected
record; all three refer to **CHEMBL509976**. This is a structural support fact,
not an activity result. There is no multi-molecule choice in this admitted
group, so it cannot demonstrate a better molecular shortlist. The fourth frozen
ghrelin case remains projection-rejected. No row was refilled or retried.

**3. Source methods add procedure anchors without a demonstrated decision change.**
Eight cases have a partial method-family draft link, one source-covered case
remains unresolved and three cases lack source methods. These are author drafts;
zero exact case mappings have independent adjudication. The accepted 5HT1A case
does not have an exact matching procedure in the admitted methods blocks. The
perforin-related case names K562 in structured metadata, while several retained
cytotoxicity procedures involve that target cell; its exact effector/procedure
remains unresolved. All seventeen null-unit records remain from a deferred paper.

**4. The pairwise controls expose a task-support problem.**
Of 66 mechanical pairs among twelve cases, 60 already have different target IDs.
Among the six same-target pairs, three contain a rejected projection, and the
other three already show different residue-context anchors in metadata. Among
the ten admitted cases there are 45 pairs: 42 different-target pairs and three
same-target residue-context pairs. These are dependent diagnostic comparisons,
not 66 or 45 independent scientific repetitions.

The control concerns unqualified same-target/same-context pooling. Different
residue anchors do not prove that biological transfer is impossible; they mean
equivalence has not been justified by an unspecified generic target label.
No source-only eligibility change was established in this audit, and no compound
ranking or policy utility was evaluated.

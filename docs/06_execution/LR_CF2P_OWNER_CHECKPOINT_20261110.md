## Research: completed design review, NO_GO_THIS_MECHANISM

Two mechanisms were considered: probability-aware chemotype coverage, and
dependence/feedback-aware confirmation selection. One final design ticket and
12 exact constructed cases were completed. No molecular fits, private CF1 rows,
new activity or final16 were opened.

The core limitation is **contribution and identifiability**, not a failed p-value
threshold. Expected coverage is already a classical objective; diverse active
search already supplies a capped multi-class reward framework. Joint dependence
and feedback can matter in constructed worlds, but that observation alone is
not a distinct computational method. Current CF1 aggregate tables cannot identify
the required joint response law or adaptive counterfactuals. A useful, distinct
strategy and suitable evidence are both missing.

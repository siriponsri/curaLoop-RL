## What changed in our understanding

1. Refitting has value: fresh full-pool G1 discovers3.875 more positives than
   frozen-cache on average. This is not a claim that every update is valuable.
2. History can locate limited headroom: UPDATE-REUSE is+0.375 in the whole
   reused paired slice and+1.1667 in the prespecified stale>=4 bin, but that bin
   has only eight states and different component coverage/case mix. It is not a
   validated context trigger or an independent biological result.
3. PLAN really changes decisions (22/24 versus UPDATE), but its mean incremental
   effect is only+0.3125 cheap /+0.4375 high with added CPU. Same-action equivalence
   does not explain its weak average usefulness. Repeated-policy benefit remains
   unidentified by one forced operation plus a G1 tail.

   CPU than G1 but loses0.53125 hits, exceeding the frozen0.25 loss preference.
   Nearest-hit is very cheap but loses1.6875; frozen-cache loses3.875. FixedT4 is
   more expensive and has fewer mean hits than G1. All arms delivered32 assays.

These are developmental findings on six assays/four annotation components,
with two fresh seeds. They do not yet support a new method superiority claim,
publication readiness, independent-family transfer, clinical utility or lab ROI.
The old M2 result is not promoted to promising by this diagnostic investigation.

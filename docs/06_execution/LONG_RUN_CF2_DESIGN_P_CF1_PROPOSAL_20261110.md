## CF2-Design: one bounded scientific decision

Question: is there a distinguishable, identifiable mechanism for selecting a
confirmation portfolio beyond the classical controls already explaining CF1?
Review at most two hypotheses, without treating either as novel in advance:

1. Primary-to-confirmation error and within-chemotype outcome dependence may
   alter marginal confirmed chemotype value beyond fixed ranks, group quotas
   and classical expected-coverage optimization.
2. Adaptation to delivered confirmation feedback across chemotypes may alter
   portfolio choices beyond established active search and batch/multifidelity
   policies. Its information and timing requirements must be feasible.

Use the existing literature folder, pinned source/metadata and public CF1
aggregates. Allow at most 24 literature lookup tool calls and 12 additional
freely accessible primary full texts, with at most 128 MiB of newly retained
reading files. Preserve DOI/version, access/read depth and exact page/section
locators. Search hits are not full-text review. Do not bypass access controls,
purchase papers or send author messages. Critical inaccessible work produces
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE with a specific owner download request if needed.

Compare directly with ranked one-per-group/round-robin quotas, expected
probabilistic coverage, strong constrained greedy/diversity controls, active
search, and applicable batch/multifidelity/dependence-aware work. A learned
probability, weighted coverage objective, agent wrapper, RL label or solver
choice alone is not a contribution. Check current official target-journal scope
as part of this bounded source review; no paid route or submission is released.

Deliver:

- Source-located closest-work matrix and an explicit established-overlap table.
- One formal objective, permissible information/state/action contract and
  causal/statistical assumptions for any surviving mechanism.
- Up to 12 small constructed falsification cases across both hypotheses;
  hand-specified probabilities only, no molecular data or fitting. Show cases
  where a cheap control is sufficient as well as any proposed distinction.
- Mandatory baselines and ablations, development/independent roles,
  dependence/uncertainty plan and a separately costed future evaluation proposal.
  Keep biological independence separate from random seeds and provider counts.
- One final ticket and claim update: GO_FOR_OWNER_PROTOCOL_REVIEW,
  NO_GO_THIS_MECHANISM or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. A GO is a design recommendation,
  not validated novelty or useful measured effect.

No private row-level CF1 data, new activity values, models, final16 features/
labels/presence or training are opened. No third hypothesis, new predictor,
mechanism replacement or automatic next experiment. If one bounded review
leaves no distinct defensible mechanism, recommend ending this algorithm line.
Do not choose a material-benefit threshold from the exposed CF1 results. If a
future confirmation study requires a hit-loss/coverage tradeoff preference, list
that decision for the owner before its protocol is released.

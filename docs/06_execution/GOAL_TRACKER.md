2. Identify direct prior art, including contrary evidence; do not claim novelty
   from WAIT, batching, multi-hit utility, synthetic training or an RL label.

   falsify it; separate theoretical targets from existing implementations.

   non-RL comparators and independent biological/operational split questions.

   no new campaign, model training, dependency installation, SSH or payment.
6. Obtain independent review and record actual checks, changed paths, commit

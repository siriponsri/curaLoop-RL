## Proposed D1 population and estimands

Use only the same six exposed FS-Mol development assays and their four historical
annotation components. The old validation two assays are already exposed and
are excluded; no final features/labels/presence, new dataset or split. Reuse the
frozen M2 RF predictor and B32, with uniform n0=0/16; initial positives excluded.
Seeds are within-task repetitions, not biological replication. No certified
family, rare-hit, latency, wet-lab cost or clinical inference follows.

First inventory the retained M2 prefix/snapshot/three-branch schemas, pins,
assigned/actual-operation support, pairing keys, continuation and clock semantics
without reading their utility values. After owner approves the estimands and
usefulness preferences, reuse these already executed comparisons wherever they
identify the question; do not regenerate branches merely to get a new result.
Their seeds, masks and original design stay historical, and no independent
replication is gained. Freeze the exact reuse manifest before inspecting its
diagnostic outcomes. Missing counterfactual paths are not imputed from traces.
If only timing/action telemetry is missing, run an outcome-free probe where
admitted rather than repeat terminal-utility campaigns. Fresh molecular units
below are an upper fallback only when the outcome-blind support audit shows a
specific missing estimand and that fallback has owner authorization. If reuse
suffices, close without dispatching the fresh matrix or consuming its reservations.

The fallback generates 12 fresh MIX prefixes (6 assays x 2 initializations x seed 71003), save
after deliveries 8 and 16: exactly 24 assigned states. No outcome-based state
selection. Record every cold, terminal or infeasible state; no replacement.
Use the existing seeded 0.5 Random / 0.5 G1 MIX implementation. This exposes
different cache ages without inventing a generator; G1-only prefixes would
usually have stale count one and poorly identify a label-count trigger.
The estimand is restricted to these MIX-generated states, not arbitrary L states.
Cold states cannot establish UPDATE/PLAN contrasts. If fewer than 12 eligible
states across at least three components remain, stop with insufficient support;
changing the schedule/cohort requires an owner decision.

At each state fork REUSE, UPDATE and PLAN using identical prefix data/cache,
predictor seed, candidate/tie order and clock cap. After one forced operation,
continue the same frozen G1. Use deterministic per-state model seeds; log branch
execution order rotated by state index to reduce order confounding. No hidden
outcome affects randomness, eligibility or resource quotes. Each branch owns its
model and evaluator oracle; no cross-branch delivered-label/cache sharing.

- H1: terminal new-positive difference UPDATE minus REUSE, at fixed total B/C,
  plus CPU/deliveries and first-action/ranking changes. Same prefix charge once
  in each hypothetical campaign. Report assigned-state and executed-support
  summaries separately, including unsupported counts and interruptions.
- H2: PLAN minus UPDATE isolates planning after a common refresh; PLAN minus
  REUSE measures the bundled operation. Report actual choice-change frequency,
  added CPU, lost delivery opportunities and full terminal utility separately.
- H3: cheap controls versus G1 under the same B/C. Contexts considered before
  outcomes: stale-label count, remaining budget, cached margin and past error.
  Ranking change after a refit is an audit variable, not a free predecision
  feature; any shadow refit must be charged. Model age in deliveries equals
  stale-label count here and is not wall-clock age.

No single CPU-to-hit exchange rate is established by the user's scientific goal.
Therefore report the joint discovery/CPU frontier, never invent net dollars.
At constrained C, loss of acquisitions supplies actual simulator opportunity
cost. At loose C, extra CPU with no lost acquisitions remains extra compute;
do not translate it into a biological gain/loss without an owner-approved utility.

## Minimum useful effect and stopping rules, proposed for owner selection

Before any diagnostic outcomes, owner must accept or change these transparent
engineering preferences: **+1 new positive per B32**, or **>=20% scientific CPU
saving with mean hit loss <=0.25** against the strongest cheap control. These
are screening preferences, not clinical or confirmatory thresholds and not
established by M2's observed effects. They must not be reduced after results.

H1/H2 within-state effects diagnose headroom; they are not a deployable oracle
policy. Positive average refresh value alone does not justify D2. D2 discussion
requires useful full-campaign control tradeoffs, component-level direction in
at least 3/4 components, and evidence that predecision context can distinguish
beneficial from nonbeneficial actions. With only 24 states, any context pattern
is exploratory and may require a separately approved diagnostic, not immediate
training. No p-value threshold or seed-count multiplication of sample size.
Report assay and component summaries and ranges; any seed interval remains
conditional on these fixed tasks. Snapshot branches are dependent within prefix.

Stop for exhausted resource/stage/deadline caps, leakage/pin mismatch, missing
pair, unsupported replay state, a new material endpoint/rights requirement, or
insufficient support. Close with an honest negative/inconclusive result if cheap
controls dominate, PLAN rarely changes choices without benefit, or usefulness
is too small/uncertain. No automatic third redesign, cohort expansion, policy
complexity increase or protected-set access to resolve ambiguity.

## What counts as enough B potential to continue

**Execution completion and potential are separate verdicts.** A correctly
completed negative or NO_GO study does not automatically release C. Conversely,
C does not require journal acceptance, proven commercial demand or a confirmatory
clinical/material-effect threshold. The owner chose estimation with uncertainty;
no new numerical significance or minimum-effect cutoff is introduced here.

The B closeout must record the following checks before the C decision:

| Gate | Evidence needed for PASS |
| --- | --- |
| Valid evidence and intended use | The data actually used have a resolved intended-use basis, explicit observation semantics and a justified population; no fabricated full-library oracle or protected-panel leakage |
| Demonstrated useful capability | At least one prespecified mechanism or workflow capability is evaluated on permitted real-source development cases against an appropriate control; fixture/schema tests alone are insufficient |
| Fair comparison and uncertainty | Freeze the primary outcome, direction, cases and controls before the pilot; report effect estimates, uncertainty, heterogeneity, errors and costs; do not select a winning endpoint after seeing results |
| Reproducibility and review | Frozen source/input identities, replayable evidence, essential ablations and a scoped review support the actual capability and its limits |
| Feasible product continuation | Name a concrete R&D job and reusable B assets, show a credible CPU/client/dependency path within C, and identify the licence/intended-use scope of each portfolio component |

Useful capability may concern better confirmation choices, better evidence/
endpoint resolution or fewer unsupported recommendations. The exact pilot
endpoint must be selected in advance. A favorable point estimate with unclear
support is not by itself a PASS. The potential verdict is an explicit technical
judgment, not a confirmatory efficacy test or an estimate of market demand.

At B closeout, produce a `B_POTENTIAL_DECISION` record with the prespecified
outcome, comparator, observed effect/uncertainty, group counts, failure cases,
cost/profile, reviewer comments, reusable assets and the C tasks they justify.

- **POTENTIAL_SUFFICIENT:** all gates pass, and the documented findings support
  a concrete useful continuation. Begin the bounded C contract and execution

- **POTENTIAL_UNCERTAIN:** evidence is too weak, conflicting or incomplete to
  make that judgment. Present options to the owner and continue only independent

- **POTENTIAL_INSUFFICIENT:** the valid comparison does not support a useful
  continuation, or a critical validity/feasibility gate fails. Close B, preserve

Research and product potential must be labelled separately. Workflow potential

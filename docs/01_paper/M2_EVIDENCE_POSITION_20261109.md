## What the study establishes

The fixed matrix and information/CPU/trace/source checks completed. All raw
runtime/data artifacts returned, were hash-reread and preceded exact remote
cleanup. The study is auditable and its negative/mixed findings are retained.

History information improves the learned gate relative to its no-history
ablation: +1.2875 positives, conditional95% interval [0.6125, 1.9625], positive
in 4/4 annotation components. Removing UPDATE also reduces discovery on average.
This supports useful state information in this implementation, not superiority
over the strongest controls or independent population generalization.

## Why it does not pass

Primary RF/Ccheap means: L 19.275, G1 19.500, T 19.700, Gk 19.5125 and E 18.1125.
L-G1=-0.225 and L-T=-0.425; only 1/4 components favors L over G1. Thus the
predeclared rule fails on effect directions and breadth. There was no p<0.05
gate or invented practical-effect margin to relax.

Mean scientific CPU is 3.170 s for L versus 2.713 s for G1 and 1.381 s for Gk.
Gk has both higher mean discovery and lower mean CPU than L in this setting;
this is descriptive mean-point dominance, not statistical population dominance.
L's advantage over E alone does not establish incremental value over strong
simple controls. Offline training/tuning and startup remain additional costs.

Planning is not established as the useful ingredient: L minus no_PLAN is
- 0.3125 in the primary setting, with conditional interval [-0.7125, 0.0625].
RF/high means are more favorable but their intervals include zero. Neighbor
cheap results and B16 transfer are unfavorable; B16 L-G1 is -1.1667 at Ccheap
and -0.7083 at Chigh. A broad predictor/budget-transfer claim is unsupported.

Training-target variation is sparse/mixed: RF/cheap PLAN has 29 positive,
32 zero and 35 negative terminal-difference targets; UPDATE has 13/67/16.
A mismatch between forced-operation/G1-tail supervision and recurring learned
decisions is a hypothesis worth examining, not a demonstrated cause. No new
fit, target, policy or dataset was introduced after observing these effects.

## Publication and product judgment

A reviewer would currently request a clearer method advantage and stronger
independent evidence before the intended contribution claim. Four annotation
components on previously exposed, assay-relative FS-Mol classification data do
not establish independent biological families, realistic HTS prevalence,
clinical utility, lab savings or business demand. More seeds alone would not
increase biological replication. Publication acceptance cannot be inferred.

The existing EN/TH research app and metadata workflow remain useful engineering
assets, independently of this failed transition. A future product can present
strong controls and source-grounded evidence honestly; learned-policy deployment
and commercial value require their own evidence. The new P1 integration and J1
results manuscript were not executed under the unmet conditional release.

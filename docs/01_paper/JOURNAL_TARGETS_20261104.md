# Journal targets and publication budget

Reviewed 4 November 2026, Asia/Bangkok, by separate journal-research subagents.
Owner fee ceiling: **USD 300**. No payment, editorial communication or submission
has occurred. These are current publisher-listed routes, not acceptance
predictions, currency conversions or guarantees of a final invoice.

## Recommended route

Prepare a stronger, narrowly scoped retrospective methods/software manuscript.
**Journal of Computer-Aided Molecular Design (JCAMD), subscription publication,
has an explicit no-APC route.** Its subject fit remains conditional: the
publisher prioritizes experimental validation and permits appropriate
retrospective validation for novel computational strategies. The current
workbench, three correlated development endpoints and same-implementer
reproduction do not automatically establish a sufficient advance.

A null finding can be publishable, but no editorial acceptance, broad
generalization, learned transfer, equivalence or laboratory savings is implied.
The 12+ family final study remains unfrozen/unexecuted. Keep hypothetical design
sensitivity separate from measured assay-replay results.

## JCIM policy discrepancy

The official indexed [guide](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=jcisd8),
updated 8 October 2026, describes Application Notes up to 5,000 words and at
least one figure. The indexed official
[editorial](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c02729) describes 4,000 words,
six months of active open development and uptake evidence for standalone tools.
Direct editorial access returned HTTP 403; full text was not retrieved. The
guide's direct body was also not reliably available. These are primary indexed
excerpts with retained access/version limits, not a resolved policy ruling.

A draft of at most 4,000 words would be conservative formatting, but would not
close eligibility. The earliest inspected local commit is `fbc8973`, dated
2 November 2026 in its original timezone (3 November Bangkok). Git dates do not
establish when a repository became public; the inspected history demonstrates
neither six months of public development nor community uptake. Calling a
localhost application a web service does not establish service sustainability.

## Submission conditions

Reconcile every manuscript claim; establish an advance over the closest tools;
make the minimum rights-aware reproducibility inputs accessible; retain all
unfavorable results and failures; obtain genuine independent reproduction where
feasible; confirm author declarations and a final no-charge route; then have
the human author approve the exact package. The [readiness report](SUBMISSION_READINESS_20261104.md)
and [review package](submission/README.md) retain the remaining conditions.
Repository publication is distinct from journal submission and acceptance.

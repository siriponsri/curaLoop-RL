## Interpretation safeguards

The strongest reason to revise the objective is the failed learned-scheduler
comparison, together with an experimentally relevant follow-up allocation
decision. It is not proof that a new selection algorithm will work.

Simple diversity-aware hit triage is already established. Both ranking plus
diversity and the diversity rule applied to competing rankers must be treated as
strong controls, not withheld components that manufacture a favorable comparison.
The proposed chemotype endpoint also cannot become a lead-series or biological
efficacy claim without additional definitions and evidence.

The supplied report explicitly lacked a recorded search date and treated several
access records as excerpts. Its evidence-grid counts are not a novelty test or
independent study count. This review verified the canonical OPAL v3 identifier
and title directly; older title variants are not independent replications.

Research records require original observation-state semantics. The existing A3
finding of zero complete selected replicate tuples in one tiny primary-only
canary neither qualifies a confirmation benchmark nor proves all published
datasets unusable. It is retained evidence for that scope.

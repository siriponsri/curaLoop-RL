## 1. Science & Technology Asia

The [official journal page](https://ph02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/scitechasia)
lists computer engineering/IT within its multidisciplinary scope, English
research articles and short communications, and no fees at submission or
publication. The [author guidance](https://ph02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/SciTechAsia/author_guidelines)
requires scientific merit, substantial support and reviewer assessment.

Assessment: a plausible Thai-published option for a sufficiently substantive
completed computational/application study. The present LR-SAR1 result is a
static eligibility stop, not such a completed comparison. The existing AgentRD
report would still need a convincing bounded contribution and policy/declaration
review. No assumption that STA is easier to enter or guarantees acceptance.

The journal site lists indexing information, but its displayed Scopus coverage
statement ends in 2025. This review does not independently certify 2026 indexing
or university-specific eligibility. Check the current authoritative index and
the owner's institutional requirements before choosing a submission route.
The inspected author page does not establish a complete current AI-use policy;
do not infer unrestricted AI drafting or interpretation permission from silence.

## 2. ECTI-CIT

The [official home page](https://ph01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/ecticit)
welcomes computer/IT theoretical and practical contributions, including system
experiments/prototypes, and states free publication and English language.
This is a plausible subject fit but not a budget-based automatic recommendation.

Its [21 September 2026 AI policy](https://ph01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/ecticit/announcement/view/2102)
permits generative AI for language editing only. It prohibits using it to
generate substantive scientific content, write major manuscript sections or
interpret results, and requires disclosure of allowed assistance.

This project has substantive AI-assisted design, analysis and reporting.
That cannot honestly be relabeled as grammar editing. Human rewriting alone
does not erase earlier substantive assistance. ECTI-CIT is therefore a
**conditional policy-fit candidate, not an immediately suitable submission**.
Any future choice must assess the actual work and disclose it accurately under
the venue's rules. No author correspondence is sent or authorized by this review.

There is also a live formatting inconsistency: the home page says 8-10 pages,
while the [current author guideline](https://ph01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/ecticit/author-guidelines)
and [submission checklist](https://ph01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/ecticit/about/submissions)
say 8-12 pages, double column. Do not promise a resolved page limit or prepare
a manuscript to fit one number without verifying the governing instruction.

## JCAMD and the owner's publication budget

The publisher identifies the Journal of Computer-Aided Molecular Design as
hybrid. Its subscription route has no APC; the listed open-access APC is
US$4,390 before applicable taxes, determined at acceptance. Thus subscription
can meet the owner's US$300 APC ceiling, subject to any funder/institutional
access mandate and checking any other optional charges. Cost fit does not
establish scientific fit or likelihood of acceptance.

Source: [official publishing options and fees](https://link.springer.com/journal/10822/how-to-publish-with-us),
publishing-options and publication-fee sections, read directly. No fee paid,
agreement signed, submission started or commercial service purchased.

The [official aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/10822/aims-and-scope)
concerns methods/applications for molecular analysis and design. It prioritizes
experimental validation but allows adequate retrospective validation for novel
methods or innovative computational strategies. Wet-lab work is therefore not
an automatic prerequisite for this solo project. The substantive question is
whether the final work advances molecular design or computational methodology,
and whether its validation supports that advance.

The present agent study is a constrained reliability application on exposed
aggregate evidence. A functioning wrapper, code release and complete results
are useful deliverables, but do not by themselves establish a novel molecular
design contribution. The final editorial assessment must address this gap and
the closest prior work, even if the typed source gate improves a task score.
Do not select a more expensive venue or force a positive method claim because
the target's subscription route is affordable.

## Interpretive constraints to retain in the manuscript

- B0 is a full deterministic reference on a computable, author-defined contract.
  Its32 pre-freeze conformance passes were known and disclosed. It is not an
  unseen natural-language benchmark or a general test of whether LLMs are useful.
- The three model arms share source facts, model, typed interface and caps.
  B1 has no executable compute action; the common tool-oriented instruction and
  terminal-only schema constrain its secondary comparison. No claim of a native
  ChemCrow/Paper2Agent reproduction or unconstrained base-model capability.
- Typed limitation-code completion is narrower than scientific explanation.
  A boundary answer containing only permitted true values is an action-contract
  failure when abstention was required, not evidence of an actual protected-data
  leak or an invented clinical recommendation.
- The256-token per-call cap, common JSON recovery and three-call limit are part
  of the tested controller. Truncated/malformed outputs must be reported beside
  numeric error rates; unparseable output is not silently counted as a correct
  claim. Scientific claim checking and task completeness are separate.
- Cost observations are conditional on this CPU runtime, execution order and
  prompt caching. There is no measured laboratory saving, energy footprint,
  commercial ROI or real-user productivity result.
- Author checking and a separately implemented CSV oracle are not independent
  expert validation. Case resampling is exploratory, conditional on this one
  corpus/model and authored tasks, and does not create biological independence.

These constraints do not authorize prompt/scoring changes after the freeze.

## Apply safely

Use the package-level Python apply helper and [apply guide](docs/06_execution/APPLY_AND_PUBLISH.md). Work in the real Git clone, inspect owner changes and use a normal push. The package does not commit/push on your behalf. Keep `reading_room_private/` outside the repository and do not commit the full ZIP.

## Status language

IMPLEMENTED means present in the inspected source. RECORDED_HISTORICAL means an existing artifact was read. PROPOSED/NOT_RUN means the work remains to be done. BUSINESS_HYPOTHESIS means no buyer or outcome validation has yet been recorded. Every future paper or sales claim must agree with the [claim ledger](docs/00_project/CLAIM_LEDGER.md).
